# Course outline

The goal is a short foundation course with enough theory to understand practical training. Lessons will be added in order.

| Module | Topic | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | [Introduction to Forex Trading](01-introduction.md) | Currency pairs, bid/ask, long/short, brokers and other markets. |
| 02 | [Essential Trading Terminology](02-trading-terminology.md) | Pips, ticks, lots, costs, leverage, margin, equity and stop-outs. |
| 03 | [Trading Sessions and Timeframes](03-trading-sessions.md) | SAST, daylight saving, liquidity, volatility and M1 considerations. |
| 04 | MetaTrader 5 | Demo setup, charts, orders, SL/TP, trailing stops, hedging and netting. |
| 05 | Technical Analysis | Candlesticks, trend, support/resistance, breakouts and multiple timeframes. |
| 06 | Technical Indicators | MA, EMA, RSI, MACD, ATR, Bollinger Bands and Fibonacci retracement. |
| 07 | Fundamental Analysis | Rates, CPI, GDP, NFP, central banks and economic calendars. |
| 08 | Risk Management | Sizing, SL/TP, R:R, break-even, drawdown, exposure, execution costs and daily limits. |
| 09 | Trading Psychology | How predefined risk and rules reduce emotional pressure; discipline and loss acceptance. |
| 10 | Simple Demo Trading Strategy | One rule-based trend-following pullback strategy; test without claims of profitability. |
| 11 | Trading Journal and Performance | Track trades, outcomes, costs, rule adherence and basic performance. |
| 12 | Universal Risk Manager EA | Strategy-independent MT5/MQL5 automatic SL/TP and protected order entry; demo validation. |

One demo strategy only: trend-following pullbacks, with clear entry/exit rules and a higher-timeframe trend check. Proposed demo parameters are M15 trend confirmation, M1/M5 entries, 1% planned equity risk, 2R target, 2% daily loss limit and at most three trades daily. Stop trading when the daily loss limit is reached; do not take an additional trade whose planned risk exceeds the remaining budget. These are illustrative testing defaults, not a profitable-system claim or a recommendation to trade live.
